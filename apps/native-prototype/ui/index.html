Resources during the scene

Game frame timing describes application rendering/presentation, not physical scanout or input latency. The 1% low uses the mean of the slowest 1% of frames. Short runs can reveal problems; small differences need repeated comparisons. Resource readings use a coarse clock estimate; they show trends and cannot identify the cause of one frame-time spike.
