How to read the comparison

Independent coverage counts each selected region separately. Joint composition assigns each unit of image area to at most one requested portion. A dark-red pixel may qualify as both red and dark, so the two models can rank images differently.

The amounts beside thumbnails are measured from each method's pixel sample for each family, with overlap allowed. They are available color area, not the final non-overlapping assignment. Check the sampling note in each column: resizing can change narrow color membership. Method errors use each method's own representation and are not human relevance scores.

These live methods currently compare hard color membership and target amounts. They do not apply the earlier editor's graded core-to-edge preference. A perfect amount score is not a guarantee that the picture looks right.

The color-family bank is experimental and untuned: some “red” matches look pink or brown. Exact ordering for the stored model does not mean the families have been visually validated.
