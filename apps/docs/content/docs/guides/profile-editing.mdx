---
title: Editing your profile
description: Update your public identity, biography, picture, and previous handles
---

## Open your profile editor

1. Sign in and open the navigation menu beside your name.
2. Choose **Your profile**.
3. Select **Edit profile** at the top right.

The editor starts with an overview in the same layout as your public profile. **View profile** shows a preview of your saved identity and biography, without the wallpaper gallery. Select **Open your profile** inside that preview to open the public page in a new tab.

## Edit your name, handle, or biography

Select the pencil beside your display name or profile handle to edit it in place. Select **Edit biography** to open the Markdown editor below your identity. Use **Write** and **Preview** to switch between authored text and its rendered appearance; **Formatting help** lists the supported features.

Select the checkmark to save, or the cross to cancel. Hover over an icon for its action label. While saving, the checkmark becomes a spinner. A successful save shows a brief green check before returning to the overview. A failed save shows a red cross and keeps your editor and draft open so you can correct or retry it. Success and failure notifications appear in the same bottom-right stack as upload notifications.

If your profile changed in another session, use **Refresh profile** in the error message. This loads the current profile and preserves your unsaved text for another save attempt.

Biography previews use the same Markdown renderer as the public page. **Refresh preview** retries embedded wallpaper previews without saving your draft. Only published wallpapers you own can be embedded; newly uploaded wallpapers may take a moment to become available.

## Profile handle cooldown and previous handles

You can change your profile handle once every seven days. During the cooldown, the pencil is disabled and a relative message such as **Available for change in 7 days** appears beside it. Hover over or focus the remaining time to see the full availability date and time.

The compact previous-handles summary opens a dialog with separate retained, expiring, and historical lists:

- **Retained aliases** redirect to your current profile handle and count toward your retained limit.
- **Expiring aliases** stop counting toward that limit immediately. Scheduling removal gives existing links another 24 hours to redirect; immediate expiration requires a separate confirmation.
- **Historical handles** may be reactivated when eligible and when a retained slot is available. The dialog explains when a handle is unavailable or its eligibility has expired.

Changing your handle can schedule older aliases for removal if the retained limit is exceeded. A confirmation identifies the affected handles before you save. Keeping or reactivating an alias does not change your current handle or its cooldown. Use **Refresh aliases** to load the latest expiry and eligibility information.

## Change your profile picture

Select the pencil on your picture to open its dialog. **Choose picture** opens the file picker and displays a local preview. Select **Upload picture** or **Replace picture** to save it. JPEG, PNG, and WebP are supported; the dialog shows the current size and pixel limits. Animated images are not supported.

Failed uploads keep the selected picture available for retry. If a version conflict requires **Refresh Profile**, select the picture again after refreshing. Removing a picture requires confirmation and restores your generated avatar; a pending account-picture import can also be cancelled.

Custom profile banners are not supported yet.
