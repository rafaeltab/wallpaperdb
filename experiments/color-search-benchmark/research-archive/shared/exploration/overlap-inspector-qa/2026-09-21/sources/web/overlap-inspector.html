Describe the colors

1,024 overlapping color regions, each with its own coverage and match quality. A pixel can belong to several regions.

Picked colors resolve to their nearest stored anchor. Each region has a fixed OKLab radius of 0.12: quality falls from 100% at its center to 50% at its edge. This prototype does not offer adjustable ranges.

Which fields contributed to the score?

          

            
All 1,024 overlapping regions
Coverage is the image area inside a region. Quality is the mean closeness of those matching pixels to that region’s anchor. Original image pixels are sampled before membership is measured; a reduced palette is not used.

            
These coverages overlap. They can add to more than 100%. A region’s area is not a disjoint share of the image, and unused regions make no direct contribution to this query. Multiple target amounts are scored independently; this prototype does not measure the union or enforce full-palette purity.
