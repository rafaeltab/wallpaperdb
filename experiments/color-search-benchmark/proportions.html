How to read this prototype
  • Absolute proportions: 40% green means 40% of the whole image. Amounts below 100% leave a remainder. Amounts above 100% are invalid; this page never silently rescales your request.
  • Default behavior: both too little and too much of a requested color matter. The optional minimum mode accepts extra requested color in the remainder.
  • Color overlap: a palette color contributes to its nearest requested color, with less credit as its shade differs. The same patch cannot fully count as both red and orange. Identical requested hex colors are merged for scoring.
  • Perceptual transport: distributes the palette’s area across the requested colors and the remainder while paying for color differences. In the default mode, putting extra requested color in the remainder also has a cost.
  • Estimated area: measured from up to 32 palette colors, not a full-resolution pixel count. Area assigned by the transport solver is not shown as measured coverage. Broad tolerance and nearby requested colors can blur the distinction.
  • Previous palette presence: rewards having the colors, with their requested amounts used as relative importance. It does not enforce absolute proportions. The comparison makes that limitation visible.
  • Nearest is not necessarily close: this small corpus may have no suitable wallpaper, especially for five-color compositions. The page still ranks every image and exposes the estimated composition.
  • Controlled examples: switch the image set to simple synthetic color strips with known proportions to see whether a 40% request correctly prefers 40% to 80%. These examples are separate from the 100 real wallpapers.

Runs locally over the recorded 100-image corpus. Query settings live in the URL. This is an experimental ranking interface; it does not alter production search.
