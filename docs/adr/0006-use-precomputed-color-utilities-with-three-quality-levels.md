# Use precomputed color utilities with three quality levels

Accepted on September 24, 2026 as the direction for a future production implementation. The user endorsed the three-position combined quality slider after comparing the prototypes. This decision preserves the preferred color-scoring behavior while moving its expensive calculations into indexing. It does not implement or approve the prototype code for production. The [research record](../research/color-querying/README.md) preserves the exploration and supporting evidence.

## Context

Color search should reflect how a wallpaper feels. A dark red background can feel strongly red, while a larger area of orange or dull pink may be a poorer match. Users also want grayscale, dark and other visual qualities, and combinations with requested proportions. Accuracy and fast responses take priority over flexible controls and storage savings. Retrieving a limited candidate set and sorting it in the gateway cannot guarantee the best results across every eligible wallpaper.

The investigation progressed from histogram vector comparisons through explicit coverage, overlapping color regions and several scoring refinements. The user preferred shade-aware matching with stricter hue admission, coverage and quality considered together, and all five cutoff layers contributing. They saw little practical difference between 256 and 1,024 bins. Query-time evaluation of the preferred formula became expensive at scale; precomputing its answers made a more promising performance trade-off.

## Decision

Use 256 overlapping color bins with the research method's shade-aware, strict-hue similarity. A bin represents an anchor color and nearby colors that count toward it. A sampled pixel may contribute to several bins. Shade tolerance gives darker versions of a chromatic color more credit, while a visibility adjustment limits credit for near-black pixels and the hue gate reduces credit for substantially different hues. Neutral targets retain their separate treatment. A picked color resolves to its nearest anchor, so this direction does not promise exact hexadecimal-color discrimination.

For each bin, measure five nested layers at minimum similarity levels of zero, 25%, 50%, 75% and 90%. The zero layer still has finite color support; it does not admit every pixel. Each layer records the fraction of the image it covers and the average similarity of its admitted pixels, called conditional quality. Broader layers describe more approximate matches; stricter layers describe smaller, more faithful matches.

Score each layer independently before combining it with the others. A vibe query rewards coverage with diminishing returns and applies the selected quality influence. A proportion query rewards closeness to the requested area, penalizes excess more strongly than shortfall, and applies a smaller quality penalty. Thus a request for 40% green aims near 40%, rather than treating it as a minimum. Combine the layer scores with normalized cutoff weights, then average the requested targets. Preserve the [documented scoring method](../research/color-querying/METHOD.md) rather than substituting a score calculated from averaged coverage and quality.

Offer one three-position quality-preference slider. The relaxed setting uses quality influence zero and cutoff weighting zero; the default favorite uses influence 0.5 and weighting one; the strict setting uses influence one and weighting three. Retain the linear quality curve. These are selected parameter pairs, not equal perceptual intervals. Quality influence controls the penalty within each layer, while cutoff weighting shifts importance toward the stricter layers. Named abstract features have no cutoff layers, so weighting does not affect them.

Support requested proportions in 10% steps. Precompute each target's complete score for the three settings, storing one vibe utility and eleven proportion utilities per setting. The prototype includes 256 color anchors and 23 named features, giving 10,044 numeric utility fields per wallpaper. A utility is a precomputed relevance score, not a measured percentage. OpenSearch applies eligibility requirements and ranks eligible records using the selected utilities. The service must not replace this with a capped candidate rerank. Use the selected prototype's native numeric scoring path for this implementation. Global bounds and other experimental executors are outside its scope.

## Production integration decisions

Confirmed on September 30, 2026 for [issue #266](https://github.com/rafaeltab/wallpaperdb/issues/266). These decisions refine the accepted direction; they do not claim that the application already implements it. Correct final values and rankings take priority, followed by performance and then responsibility split. The [selected linked prototype](https://github.com/rafaeltab/wallpaperdb/blob/30edcb2a61e6c4cc915a61807210a3ab5e924d96/experiments/color-search-benchmark/exploration/LINKED-STRICTNESS-PROTOTYPE.md) is the behavioral and numerical reference, including its sampling, named features, float32 utility values, and OpenSearch aggregation order. Near-tie parity is judged against that numeric path rather than a differently grouped evaluation of the original formula.

Preserve every requested target contribution. Group identical complete utility keys and weight each group by its multiplicity divided by the original target count, using the researched [multiplicity correction](https://github.com/rafaeltab/wallpaperdb/blob/30edcb2a61e6c4cc915a61807210a3ab5e924d96/experiments/color-search-benchmark/exploration/FAVORITE-MULTIPLICITY.md). This corrects clause collapse without changing the intended mean or composition semantics. Rank all eligible records, including zero-score records, by descending relevance and ascending wallpaper ID for ties. Retain the existing pagination contract.

Retain versioned image measurements through NATS so utility values can be derived without decoding the original again when the measurement definition is unchanged. Measurements describe matching area and conditional quality; utilities are relevance scores derived from them. Begin with Color Extractor producing measurements and Gateway deriving utilities during projection. The implementation may change that computation split if correctness or performance warrants it, within the repository's context boundaries. A new database is not required by this decision; the existing event-retention policy remains subject to [#162](https://github.com/rafaeltab/wallpaperdb/issues/162).

Replace the complete color query contract, histogram extraction, and histogram search representation. There are no production or external consumers requiring compatibility. The maintainer will recreate infrastructure and storage and upload images again, so migration, backfill, operator rebuild tooling, parallel indexes, and staged cutover are unnecessary for this work. Durable facts, replay-safe consumers, and exclusion of incomplete color records remain correctness requirements. The current single-color picker will send a default-quality vibe query; the advanced picker remains separate in [#36](https://github.com/rafaeltab/wallpaperdb/issues/36).

## Consequences and limits

This exchanges indexing work and storage for simpler queries and gives up independently adjustable sliders and intermediate percentages. It preserves the favorite midpoint and the supported grayscale and other named queries. Measurements overlap: they describe each target separately, not a disjoint palette allocation. They do not establish an additional penalty for unwanted colors or solve every grayscale-plus-accent intention. The frozen linked prototype retains its historical duplicate-clause defect; the production integration applies the correction above and tests that case explicitly.

The [evidence](../research/color-querying/EVIDENCE.md) supports this direction, not a production capacity guarantee. The linked prototype contains 523 real wallpapers and preserves the corresponding reference evaluation outcomes. Million-record measurements used synthetic records derived from that corpus and a different, favorite-only utility bank. The full three-setting bank's million-record storage, mixed-setting concurrency and production behavior remain unmeasured. The previous 56.05 GB measurement must not be relabeled as this bank's size. A million-record capacity campaign or an invented traffic target is not a completion gate for #266. Deployment-scale performance and recovery evidence belongs to the persistent environment work in [#240](https://github.com/rafaeltab/wallpaperdb/issues/240). The [production guidance](../research/color-querying/PRODUCTION-QUESTIONS.md) distinguishes settled correctness decisions from that deferred validation.
