# Wallpaper

**Wallpaper**:
A contribution with its own identity, contributor and publication facts. Its associated Asset has a distinct identity and owns its immutable Sources.

**Wallpaper Asset reference**:
The confirmed Asset identity associated with a Wallpaper. It contains no storage coordinates.

**Creation intent**:
A creator's durable request to create one Wallpaper and obtain its Asset. Repeating that request preserves the Wallpaper and its intended Asset creation.

**First Source intent**:
The current candidate designated to complete a Wallpaper's initial content contribution. An earlier candidate's confirmation cannot complete a superseding intent.

**Wallpaper publication**:
The committed fact that the Wallpaper has its confirmed Asset and first accepted Source. Public discovery independently joins this fact with Asset delivery availability.

**Contributor**:
The authenticated creator recorded by Wallpaper, distinct from Asset's configured Owner authority.
