All 4096 color bins
The histogram uses a 128 × 128 image sample and 16 × 16 × 16 RGB cells. Only occupied bins are stored; empty bins are implicit zeros. The full-resolution image is a visual reference. Hex swatches are rounded representatives of the fractional RGB centers.
Select a square or a table row to inspect it. Pixel counts refer to the sample.

            

            

            
Keyboard: arrow keys move one bin or one row; Home/End move within a row. Enter selects a bin. RGB order: red, then green, then blue.

            
Leave-one-bin-out effects are not additive contributions. The service removes a bin and rescales the remaining image to 100%. The effect is the full score minus that new score: positive means the bin supports the score; negative means it reduces it. Effects do not sum to the wallpaper’s score. Removing the only occupied bin has no defined remaining image.
