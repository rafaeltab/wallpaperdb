---
title: Editing your profile
description: Change your public identity, biography, picture, and previous handles.
---

Sign in, open the navigation menu beside your name, choose **Your profile**, then **Edit profile**. Select the pencil beside your name, handle, or picture, or choose **Edit biography**.

Save with the checkmark or cancel with the cross. Failed saves keep your draft available. If another session changed your profile, **Refresh profile** loads its current state while preserving unsaved text for another attempt.

## Biography

Use **Write** and **Preview** to check your Markdown. **Formatting help** lists supported features. Preview uses the same renderer as your public profile.

Embed a wallpaper with `![Alt text](wallpaper:<wallpaper-id>)`. Only your own published wallpapers can be embedded; a new upload may take a moment to become available. Ordinary image URLs and raw HTML are not supported. External links require HTTPS.

**Refresh preview** retries unavailable wallpaper previews without saving your draft.

## Handles and old links

You can change your handle once every seven days. The editor shows when the cooldown ends. Previous handles appear in separate lists:

- Retained aliases redirect to your current profile and count toward the retained limit.
- Expiring aliases stop counting toward that limit immediately. Scheduling removal preserves redirects for another 24 hours. Immediate expiration needs a separate confirmation.
- Historical handles can be reactivated while eligible and while a retained slot is available. The dialog explains unavailable handles and expired eligibility.

A handle change can schedule older aliases for removal when the retained limit is exceeded. The confirmation identifies affected handles. Keeping or reactivating an alias does not change your current handle or its cooldown. Use **Refresh aliases** for current expiry and eligibility information.

## Pictures

Choose, drop, or paste a JPEG, PNG, WebP, or GIF picture. Adjust the crop and zoom in the local preview, then choose **Apply** to upload it. GIF animation keeps its timing, looping, and transparency when applied.

Failed uploads keep the selected picture and crop available for retry. If another session changed your profile, refreshing the saved profile preserves your selection and crop for another attempt. Removing a picture restores your generated avatar and cancels any pending account-picture import. Already downloaded or cached copies of a removed picture cannot be recalled.
