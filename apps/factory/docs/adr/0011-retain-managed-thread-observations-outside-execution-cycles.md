# Retain managed thread observations outside execution cycles

Repeated environment scans and historical activity reads delayed planning even when capacity was available. Following [issue #819](https://github.com/rafaeltab/wallpaperdb/issues/819), the factory consumes persistent local observations while service-owned background synchronization acquires shared metadata and detailed activity for managed threads. This accepts observation lag in exchange for execution cycles that do not wait for T3 catch-up; missing or disconnected observations cannot establish inactivity, release reservations or authorize completion.

Management follows retained job and recovery ownership, including implementation before a PR, archived threads and descendants below finished parents. Detailed observation ends only after verified landing and idle outstanding responsibilities. Independent complete lightweight inventory refreshes maintain Project links every 15 minutes without determining admission readiness. The [observer](../../src/adapters/t3/observations.ts), [atomic checkpoint adapter](../../src/adapters/store/checkpoints.ts) and [composition](../../src/composition/implementation.ts) implement acquisition and restart recovery while preserving the authority and uncertain-outcome rules in [ADR 0002](0002-preserve-claims-across-uncertain-external-outcomes.md) and [ADR 0008](0008-reconcile-completion-from-versioned-agent-state.md).
