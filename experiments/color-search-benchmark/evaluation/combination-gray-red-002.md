## Presentation and selection

Two published photographs were manually sourced and visually inspected for red accents against neutral surroundings. The roses return as a familiar comparison. Selection is a hypothesis about useful examples; no image is pre-labeled as good or ideal.

A–C are new presentation labels. Complete source images are contained on the same neutral background, with up to 340 CSS pixels of height. Images are not cropped or locally reencoded. They have different aspect ratios, and the files are larger than the earlier thumbnails. The roses are now displayed from their original PNG, so this is not an identical-presentation repeatability test.

These are different scenes, not controlled edits of one image. Preferences cannot isolate the effect of amount, red strength, background tone or spatial arrangement on their own.
