---
name: do-work
description: Implement one issue in the supplied checkout, review it, and publish a PR.
---

Use the existing checkout. If given a predecessor PR, stack your PR on it.

1. **Understand.** Read the issue and comments, inspect the relevant code, and clarify missing requirements. Follow the [issue workflow](../../../../docs/agents/issue-tracker.md) for claiming, dependency readiness, and `In Progress` status, and [task scope](../../../../docs/agents/task-scope.md) for authorization and retained decisions.
2. **Plan.** Make an implementation checklist. Record the starting comparison and originating requirements for final review.
3. **Implement and commit.** Use $tdd and $development-principles. Work in small increments; check, format, and commit each before starting the next.
4. **Validate.** Run [`make ci`](../../../../Makefile) and fix failures. Use $testing for focused checks and E2E guidance. Mutation-pilot checks are advisory and excluded from completion requirements. Report available outcomes and proceed without waiting or retrying merely to turn the pilot green. Build baselines in CI; local mutation diagnostics require a concrete question and at most five minutes overall including shutdown. Ordinary CI remains mandatory. An issue's explicit acceptance-verification policy may replace the usual TDD loop for mutation tooling.
5. **Review and fix.** Run separate read-only subagents in parallel, one using $code-review and one using $review-agent, against the same complete issue change. Pass the recorded originating requirements as the spec source. For production behavior, tests, or helpers, gather the surrounding implementation and owning suites under $code-review and assess test meaning as well as execution. Compare against the predecessor PR when supplied, otherwise the default base. Include the task-scope context in both briefs and every repeated round. Record comparisons and results. Verify findings under task scope, fix confirmed in-scope issues with new commits, and repeat required validation and affected reviews until no required findings or scope decisions remain pending. For reviewed-work or base-branch changes, assess prior evidence before dispatching another round; carry forward applicable evidence and review material or unresolved changes with their interactions. Report user-deferred proposals separately. Unavailable or failed required reviewers leave this step incomplete.
6. **Open a PR.** Use $pr and $github-markdown-bodies to publish automatically, stacking on the predecessor PR when supplied. Include the issue's closing reference, validation, and review results. For default-branch PRs, verify the issue in `closingIssuesReferences` before handoff. Record feature demonstrations and attach them with $pr-video-attachment, or explain why a recording is inapplicable. Link the PR in T3 and record its URL and tested commit in the issue. Verify `In Review` for ready PRs; drafts stay `In Progress`. Leave the issue open until landing.
